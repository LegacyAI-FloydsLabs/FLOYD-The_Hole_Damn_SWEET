# Five-Seat Deliberation

The value is structured disagreement. Every seat speaks; seats with diverging
incentives must actually diverge.

Per seat, one pass in fixed order — CTO, CFO, COO, CISO, CPO — each delivers:
- POSITION: which option (including "do nothing") it backs.
- TOP-3 REASONS from its domain lens only (CTO=feasibility/debt,
  CFO=cost/opportunity, COO=execution/sequencing, CISO=security/compliance/
  blast-radius, CPO=user value/timing/reversibility).
- MOST-FEARED RISK: the single failure it weights highest.
- MIND-CHANGER: the evidence that would flip its position.

Anti-groupthink rules:
- If all five agree on a contested question, the deliberation is broken —
  reassign the two seats with the most divergent incentives to argue the
  strongest opposing case and re-run.
- Assumptions are labeled; numbers cite a source or are tagged estimates with
  their basis; sunk costs are named as sunk and excluded from the forward
  decision.

Then cross-examine the two most opposed seats and keep what survives. The
chair's synthesis records the recommendation AND the dissent register — no
seat's objection is deleted to make the memo tidy.
